Notifications · Screen inventory

Every screen and state of the notification bell and the right-hand panel, laid out flat for the HTML to Figma plugin. The panel is shown at its real size (420 × 900). The three states are Empty, Normal and Incoming; the preview switch used to move between them is left out.
